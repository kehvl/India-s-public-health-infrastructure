# Health Dynamics of India (2023–24) — Interactive Dashboard

An interactive single-file web dashboard presenting key health infrastructure and human resource statistics for India as on **31 March 2024**, sourced from the **Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MOHFW), Statistics Division** report (*Health Dynamics of India: Infrastructure & Human Resources, 2023–24*).

## Features

- **National Overview**: At-a-glance KPI cards for Sub-Centres (SC), Primary Health Centres (PHC), Community Health Centres (CHC), Sub-District Hospitals (SDH), District Hospitals (DH), and Medical Colleges, paired with facility distribution charts, rural population coverage norms vs. actual averages, and national HR summaries.
- **Infrastructure**: Rural, urban, and tribal facility breakdowns, medical college counts, and notes on PM-ABHIM and Ayushman Arogya Mandirs[cite: 1].
- **Human Resources**: Specialist and support staff comparisons across rural, urban, and tribal facilities[cite: 1].
- **State Profiles**: Dropdown selector to inspect district/village counts, facilities, and CHC/PHC staffing for supported states/UTs and the All-India total[cite: 1].
- **State Comparison**: Interactive metric and area filters with a horizontal bar chart and comparison table[cite: 1].
- **Rural vs. Urban Analysis**: Share comparisons and staffing contrasts across rural, urban, and tribal settings[cite: 1].
- **Full Data Table & Exports**: Searchable state-wise table, print-friendly layout (`@media print`), and one-click **CSV** and **JSON** downloads[cite: 1].

## Usage

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open `india_health_dashboard.html` in any modern web browser (an active internet connection is recommended to load Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and Chart.js from their CDNs)[cite: 1].

## Data & Scope Note

- **Reference Date**: 31 March 2024[cite: 1].
- **Source**: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MOHFW), Government of India[cite: 1].
- **Dataset Coverage**: The embedded dataset includes the **All-India total** and **11 representative States/UTs** (Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Assam, Chhattisgarh, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, and Uttarakhand)[cite: 1].
