Scope of This Dashboard

This interactive dashboard visualises data from the Health Dynamics of India 2023–24 report published by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare. It covers health infrastructure (Sub-Centres, PHCs, CHCs, SDHs, DHs, Medical Colleges) and human resources (doctors, nurses, specialists, paramedical staff) deployed at these facilities, broken down by state/UT and by rural, urban, and tribal classification. Data is as on 31 March 2024.

The report covers 28 States and 8 Union Territories, with 757 districts and 664,668 villages. The dashboard currently includes 11 representative states plus the All-India total. The full report (272 pages, 117+ detailed statistical tables) includes all states.
